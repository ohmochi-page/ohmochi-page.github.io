Note: Digital Tasbih Counter Pro uses Google Account for sign-in, cloud backup, and subscriptions. We do not delete your Google Account itself. This only applies to data stored by Digital Tasbih Counter Pro (local + Firebase cloud + RevenueCat).

Option 1 — Delete Directly In the App (Fastest, for signed-in users)

  1. Open Digital Tasbih Counter Pro → Account.
  2. Tap the delete (trash) icon next to your profile.
  3. Confirm twice. The App will: delete your cloud backup (users/{uid} in Firestore), delete your Firebase login, and clear that account's local data on the device.
  4. If re-authentication is required (old session), follow the Google prompt once, then repeat step 2.

Option 2 — Delete via Email Request

  1. Cancel subscription (if any) via Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Digital Tasbih Counter Pro → Cancel subscription, or via web: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
  2. Request deletion by emailing ohmochisukabumi@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Subject: Delete Digital Tasbih Counter Pro data.
  3. Guest users: just clear app data or uninstall — no cloud data exists to delete.

Data Deleted

  • Cloud backup: dzikir list, counter values, theme, preferences, email in Firestore users/{uid}
  • Firebase Authentication login for this App
  • Account local data on the device
  • App preferences
  • Subscription records in RevenueCat (forwarded on request)
  • Usage analytics linked to your account

Data Not Deleted

  • Your Google Account itself
  • Google transaction logs
  • Data required by law

Retention

  • In-app deletion: immediate (cloud + login removed at once)
  • Email requests: processed within 30 days
  • Confirmation email sent after completion
