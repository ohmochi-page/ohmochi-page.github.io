📿 Digital Tasbih Counter Pro

by OhMochi — Professional digital dhikr counter for Android

About the App

Digital Tasbih Counter Pro is a professional digital dhikr counter that mimics a physical tasbih — casing + digital LCD + ADD / MIN / RES buttons — with click sounds, vibration feedback, and local persistence.

  • Count dhikr with targets, custom dhikr list management, and archiving
  • Themes, background variants, custom sounds, night mode, and fullscreen counter
  • Works offline as guest; optional Google Sign-In enables cloud backup & sync across your devices
  • Package: com.ohmochi.digital_tasbih_counter_pro

Why the App Requests Google User Data

When you choose Sign in with Google, the App receives your basic Google profile (name, email address, and profile photo) via Firebase Authentication to identify your account and secure your backup. Your dhikr data (counts, dzikir list, theme, preferences) is stored in Cloud Firestore under users/{uid} solely to restore and sync across your own devices. Guest mode needs no Google data at all.

Scope used: email / profile / openid (sign-in only). Full details are in the Privacy Policy below.
